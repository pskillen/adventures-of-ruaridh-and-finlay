# Setting: Space

A collection of space-themed settings for adventures beyond Earth. Mix and match as needed — not every element applies to every story.

---

## The Family Rocket

* A small, cosy rocket ship that belongs to the family — think a caravan or campervan, but pointed and with a porthole window in every room.
* Inside: a round cockpit with big chunky buttons and dials (Dad loves these), a snug sleeping cabin with bunk pods, a tiny kitchen with magnetic cups so drinks don't float away.
* Outside: painted red and white, with a wobbly antenna on top and a little door with a round handle.
* The rocket makes a low, friendly **hummm** when it flies and a cheerful **WHOOSH** on take-off.
* Max has his own small pressurised dog hatch he can peer out of.

---

## Planet Zumba (Julia Donaldson–style alien world)

* A bright, round planet with two moons — one pink, one stripy green.
* Landscape: rolling hills of soft purple grass, giant candy-striped mushrooms big enough to shelter under, and a river that flows uphill (very slowly, so it's not alarming).
* Inhabitants: the **Zumblings** — round, bouncy creatures with four eyes and exactly three hairs on top of their heads. They come in pairs — one always pink, one always yellow — and they finish each other's sentences.
* The Zumblings communicate in rhyming couplets and are very fond of swapping small round objects (pebbles, buttons, acorns).
* Nothing dangerous here — the worst thing that can happen is accidentally sitting on a mushroom and bouncing a little.

---

## The Moon

* Earth's moon, but with the gentle magic of a children's story.
* Surface: soft silver dust that puffs up in little clouds with each footstep. Quiet and still; voices sound slightly echo-y.
* Earth is always visible in the sky — enormous, blue-green, reassuring.
* The moon has one feature: a single small crater with a round flat stone in the middle that looks exactly like a table. Families of moonworms (like very slow, very tiny glowing caterpillars) live under it.
* Temperature feels chilly but not cold — like a fridge door open, not a freezer.

---

## A Friendly Space Station

* A large ring-shaped station, slowly spinning, orbiting above Earth.
* Inside: wide corridors with handrails, big observation windows, a science lab full of interesting-but-safe experiments (growing seeds in zero gravity, prisms making rainbows, etc.).
* The station is run by a crew of patient, kind astronauts who are always very pleased to have visitors and always offer a biscuit.
* In low gravity, everything is slightly floaty — not scary, just funny. Crumbs drift. Max's ears lift.
* Has a docking bay where the family rocket can clip in with a satisfying **CLUNK**.
