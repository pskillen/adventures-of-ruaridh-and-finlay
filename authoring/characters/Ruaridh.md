# Character: Ruaridh

## Core Identity

* Name: Ruaridh
* Age: Born May 2023 (infer as necessary)
* Role: The main protagonist. An observant, proud, and relatable young boy navigating the world.
* Relationships: Finlay's older brother, Max's owner, child to Bethan and Patrick
* Appearance: Short, slightly curly or wavy light-brown to dark-blonde hair. Large blue eyes and a round face with prominent, full, rosy
  cheeks.

## Development & Skills

* Vocabulary: Highly advanced for his age. He understands complex concepts when explained gently.
* Reading/Maths: Highly interested in learning to read. Understands that letters and words carry meaning. Can confidently count to 5.
* Interests: Gentle adventures, fairytale magic, exploring nature.
* Loves "fixing" things: poking it with a screwdriver, hitting it with a hammer, measuring with a measuring tape, checking it with a "
  level" (spirit level)

## Personality

* Strong and proud, but still a young kid figuring things out.
* Relatable; he sometimes needs encouragement to overcome mild obstacles.
* Loves having a "job" or a task to accomplish.
* Loves Finlay, loves the role of being a big brother.
