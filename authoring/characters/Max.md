# Max

## Core Identity

* Name: Max
* Species: Dog (Spaniel).
* Role: The loyal, energetic, and comforting sidekick.
* Appearance: Black and white, long furry tail, floppy black ears.

## Traits

* Always eager for a walk or an adventure.
* Has an excellent nose for finding things (useful for solving gentle mysteries).
* A comforting presence when the kids need reassurance.
* Don't anthropomorphise - Max can't speak, but he can "woof" "bark" or "ruff". He may also think in english in speech bubbles, or optionally woof or bark.
