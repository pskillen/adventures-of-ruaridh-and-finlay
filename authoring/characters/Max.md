# Character: Max

## Core Identity

* Name: Max
* Species: Dog (Spaniel).
* Role: The loyal, energetic, and comforting sidekick.
* Appearance: Black and white spaniel with medium-length fluffy fur. Solid black, long, floppy ears and black patches over his eyes, divided
  by a distinct white blaze running down the center of his face and snout. Body is predominantly white but heavily covered in black
  ticking (small black spots and roaning) throughout the coat.

## Traits

* Always eager for a walk or an adventure.
* Has an excellent nose for finding things (useful for solving gentle mysteries).
* A comforting presence when the kids need reassurance.
* Don't anthropomorphise - Max can't speak, but he can "woof" "bark" or "ruff". He may also think in english (in text, or speech bubble in
  image), or optionally woof or bark.
