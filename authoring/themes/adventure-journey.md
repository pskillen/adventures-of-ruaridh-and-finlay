# Themes: Adventure Journey

## Theme: The Gentle Walk

* Structure: The "Walk and Accomplish" pattern.
* The Setup: Ruaridh and Max (and sometimes Dad/Mum) set out from the house with a specific, simple goal (e.g., deliver a letter to the
  moon, find the source of a glowing light, collect 5 magic acorns).
* The Journey: The walk transitions from the familiar (the street/garden) into a gentle, fairytale-like magical realism (e.g., the woods
  turn into a glowing forest, or animals can talk).
* The Climax: A mild obstacle requires Ruaridh to use his skills (counting, recognizing a letter, or just being brave) to succeed.
* The Resolution: The task is accomplished, the magical friends say thank you, and the group walks back home, feeling proud and ready for
  sleep.
