# Audience: Stories for Ruaridh

## Target Audience: Ruaridh (Nearly 3)

* Vocabulary Strategy: Do not dumb down the language too much. Use rich, descriptive words, but ensure the context makes the meaning clear.
* Interactive Elements: Frequently include moments where Ruaridh (the listener) can participate. Ask him to count items (up to 5) or identify a letter/shape in the story.
* Bedtime Specifics: If the prompt specifies a "bedtime story," ensure the pacing actively winds down. The characters should get progressively sleepier, leading to a cozy return to bed.
