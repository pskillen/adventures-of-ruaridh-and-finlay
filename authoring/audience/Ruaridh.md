# Audience: Stories for Ruaridh

## Target Audience: Ruaridh (Nearly 3)

* Vocabulary Strategy: Do not dumb down the language too much. Use rich, descriptive words, but ensure the context makes the meaning clear.
* Interactive Elements: Sometimes include moments where Ruaridh (the listener) can participate.
  * Ask him to count items (up to 5)
* Bedtime Specifics: If the prompt specifies a "bedtime story," ensure the pacing actively winds down. The characters should get
  progressively sleepier, leading to a cozy return to bed.

### Word count

* Words per page: 10 to 30 words (roughly 20 to 60 words per two-page spread).
* Sentences per page: 1 to 3 short, active sentences.
* Paragraphs per page: 1 maximum. Text is rarely structured in formal paragraphs at this age; it's better presented as distinct, rhythmic
  lines or single blocks of text.
* Total book word count: 200 to 500 words across a standard 32-page layout (or adjust accordingly)
