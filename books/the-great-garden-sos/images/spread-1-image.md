# Spread 1 — Dad at his radio set, family arrives

**For placeholder:** Dad at his radio set, family arrives at shed door

**Prompt for Gemini:**

> Generate a picture-book illustration: interior/exterior of a cosy wooden **garden shed**, door open wide. **Dad** (enthusiastic young father in a practical jacket) sits at a cluttered workbench with a **radio set** — dials, wires, a microphone, and a **silver antenna** poking out the window. He looks delighted and surprised by a crackling noise. At the shed door: **Ruaridh** (nearly-three toddler boy, boots on wrong feet, curious face), **Mum** (kind young woman with Finlay on her hip — Finlay is a tiny baby bundled happily), and **Max** (black and white spaniel, floppy black ears, long fluffy tail, mid-skid through the door, very excited). Morning sunlight floods in from the garden behind them. Bright cheerful watercolor style, energetic and fun.
