# Spread 5 — The whole family in the sunny garden, celebrating

**For placeholder:** Ruaridh muddy-kneed and grinning, whole family together in the sunny garden

**Prompt for Gemini:**

> Generate a picture-book illustration: the **whole family together in a sunny garden**, happy and satisfied after the adventure. **Ruaridh** stands centre, proudly muddy at the knees, grinning with great satisfaction. **Dad** crouches beside him, hand on his shoulder, beaming. **Mum** stands close, warm smile. **Max** (black and white spaniel, floppy ears, fluffy tail) leans his whole body against Ruaridh's legs in a dog-hug. In the foreground, **Finlay** (tiny baby) sits on his blanket, eyes wide, both fists waving in delight. In the background, the big rhubarb and the leaf pile are visible — just a hint of small round hedgehog shapes safely tucked inside the leaves. Everything feels warm, accomplished, and perfectly Saturday. Bright watercolor and pencil, full of sunshine.
