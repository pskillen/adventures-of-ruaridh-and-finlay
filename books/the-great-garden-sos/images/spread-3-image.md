# Spread 3 — Ruaridh under the rhubarb, four baby hedgehogs visible

**For placeholder:** Ruaridh kneels under the rhubarb, four hedgehog shapes visible

**Prompt for Gemini:**

> Generate a picture-book illustration: **Ruaridh** (toddler boy, knees in soft earth, expression of amazed discovery) kneels under enormous **rhubarb leaves** and peers at a **terracotta pot lying on its side**. Around the pot are **four small round baby hedgehogs** — soft, spiky, charming in a children's-book illustrated style (not photo-realistic). In the soil beside the pot is a white garden label with a clear, large letter **H** printed on it. **Dad** crouches beside Ruaridh, gently pointing at the hedgehogs. **Max** sits just behind them, ears up, tail still, very satisfied. Garden colours: rich greens, terracotta, dark soil. Bright and warm, no scary elements. Watercolor and pencil style.
