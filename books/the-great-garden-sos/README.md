# The Great Garden SOS

## Audience

- **Primary reader/listeners:** Ruaridh (nearly 3) and Finlay (baby)
- **Tone:** Daytime — high energy, curious, warm and satisfying resolution
- See also: `authoring/audience/common.md`, `authoring/audience/RUARIDH.md`

## Theme

- **Dad's gadgets + nature mystery:** Dad's amateur radio crackles with a garden signal; the family follows Max's nose through the vegetable patch to discover a family of baby hedgehogs in a fallen terracotta pot.
- Mum's "doctor calm" gives her the plan to fix the problem.

## Characters

- Ruaridh, Finlay (on a blanket, watching), Max, Mum (Bethan), Dad (Patrick)

## Files

- **`BOOK.md`** — manuscript (10 print pages, 5 spreads; page breaks marked)
- **`images/*.png`** — final art (placeholders referenced from `BOOK.md`)
- **`images/image-context.md`** — session seed / consistency context for image generation
- **`images/spread-*-image.md`** — per-spread copy-paste prompts for Gemini / Nano Banana 2

## Interactive read-aloud ideas

- Count the baby hedgehogs aloud: **one, two, three, four**.
- Spot the letter **H** on the garden label (H for hedgehog!).
