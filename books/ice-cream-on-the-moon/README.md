# Ice Cream on the Moon

## Audience

- **Primary reader/listeners:** Ruaridh (nearly 3) and Finlay (baby)
- **Tone:** Daytime — energetic, funny, triumphant, warm resolution
- See also: `authoring/audience/common.md`, `authoring/audience/RUARIDH.md`

## Theme

- **Space adventure:** Ruaridh and Finlay fly the family rocket to the Moon Ice Cream Shop. Halfway there, the Boost Pipe comes loose. Ruaridh fixes it (tightening 3 bolts, finding the O-shaped connector), and they land in time for ice cream.
- No Mum or Dad in this story — the boys and Max are on their own.

## Settings used

- `authoring/settings/space.md` → The Family Rocket, The Moon

## Characters

- Ruaridh (Captain), Finlay (Co-pilot, in bouncy chair), Max (drifting in goldfish-bowl space helmet)

## Interactive read-aloud ideas

- Count the **3 bolts** Ruaridh tightens: one, two, three.
- Spot the letter **O** on the connector (round, like a mouth saying "oh!").

## Notes

- Max wears a goldfish-bowl space helmet throughout. He drifts/floats in low gravity. He can bark/ruff/woof but not speak.
- Finlay's reactions (kicks, arm-waves, bubble-blows) are the baby equivalent of full emotional responses — play these up for the audience.
