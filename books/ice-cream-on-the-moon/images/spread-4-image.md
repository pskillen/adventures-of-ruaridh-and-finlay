# Spread 4 — Ice cream on the moon

**For placeholder:** Ruaridh, Finlay and Max outside the Moon Ice Cream Shop, eating ice cream

**Prompt for Gemini:**

> Generate a picture-book illustration: **exterior on the moon's surface**. The ground is soft silver-grey dust. In the background: a small, round, cosy building — the **Moon Ice Cream Shop** — with a glittery sign and a welcoming warm light inside. Above it, **Earth** hangs in the dark sky — enormous, blue-green, round, beautiful. In the foreground: **Ruaridh** (nearly-three toddler, dark-blond wavy hair, big blue eyes, rosy cheeks, white astronaut suit, captain's hat too big) sits on the silver ground eating **two scoops of ice cream**, looking perfectly content. Beside him, **Finlay** (tiny baby, wispy dark-brown hair, big blue-grey eyes, tiny astronaut suit) looks absolutely astonished by a tiny lick of ice cream. **Max** (black and white spaniel, clear round goldfish-bowl space helmet) sits beside them with a wagging tail, ice cream having been dispensed through a slot in his helmet; a small puff of silver moon-dust rises from his tail-wagging. Everything is happy, warm, triumphant, and slightly magical. Bright watercolor and pencil style — the warmest, most satisfying image in the book.
