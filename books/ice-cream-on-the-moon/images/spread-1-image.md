# Spread 1 — Exterior establishing shot

**For placeholder:** The red and white rocket in space, exterior shot

**Prompt for Gemini:**

> Generate a picture-book illustration: exterior view of a **small, cosy red and white rocket ship** flying through space. The rocket is rounded and friendly — like a campervan crossed with a rocket — painted cherry red and white, with a **wobbly antenna** on top, round porthole windows, and a little round door. Through one porthole you can just make out **Ruaridh** (toddler in an astronaut suit, captain's hat too big) at the controls; through another porthole floats **Max** (black and white spaniel) wearing a **clear round fishbowl space helmet**. Space behind them: deep dark blue-black with lots of warm bright stars. **Earth** is visible in the distance behind the rocket, blue-green and round. The **moon** is faintly visible ahead. Warm, cheerful, cosy energy — this is a family adventure, not scary sci-fi. Bright watercolor and pencil style.
