# Spread 3 — The rocket fixed, zooming through space

**For placeholder:** Rocket glowing back to life, Ruaridh cheering at controls, stars streaking past portholes

**Prompt for Gemini:**

> Generate a picture-book illustration: **interior cockpit** of the rocket, now fixed and zooming fast. Through the round porthole windows, stars have stretched into long white streaks — the rocket is going very fast. On the dashboard, three small lights have turned **green**. **Ruaridh** (toddler, dark-blond wavy hair, big blue eyes, round rosy cheeks, white astronaut suit, captain's hat brim on his ears) sits at the controls with both arms raised triumphantly, mouth open in a cheer. **Finlay** (tiny baby in astronaut suit, in his bouncy chair) has both arms thrown wide open. **Max** (black and white spaniel, clear goldfish-bowl helmet) has his ears lifting straight up from the speed — this looks very funny; give him a surprised but pleased expression. Warm excited lighting. Bright watercolor and pencil style — joyful, triumphant, funny.
