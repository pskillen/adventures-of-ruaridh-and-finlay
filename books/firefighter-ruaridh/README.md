- **Finlay** — baby brother, watching from the pram
- **Max** — the spaniel, sniffing along on patrol
- **Mum (Beth)** — handing out marshmallow sticks at the campfire
- **Dad (Paddy)** — builds the campfire at the end

## Story summary

Ruaridh takes his firefighter duties very seriously. He marches around the garden with the hose, soaking trees and checking every bush for fires. Max helps. Finlay watches, wide-eyed. The garden is thoroughly inspected — and completely safe. Then Dad lights a small campfire. Ruaridh looks at it, considers his hose... and decides *this* fire is a good one. The whole family toasts marshmallows together.

## Notes
