# Firefighter Ruaridh

![[Illustration: Ruaridh in firefighter helmet with garden hose]](./images/cover.png)

-----------

## Spread 1 — On Duty

Ruaridh had a very important job today.

He pulled on his red helmet.
He picked up the garden hose.
He turned the tap.

**Whoooooosh!**

*Firefighter Ruaridh was on duty.*

![[Illustration: Ruaridh turning on garden tap, hose shooting water]](./images/spread-1.png)

-----------

## Spread 2 — The Apple Tree

He marched straight to the apple tree.

**Splosh! Splosh! Splosh!**

The water soaked every single branch.

Max sniffed carefully all the way around the bottom.

No fire here!

![[Illustration: Ruaridh hosing the apple tree, Max sniffing around base]](./images/spread-2.png)

-----------

## Spread 3 — The Rose Bush

Next came the rose bush.

**Ssssssss!** went the hose.

Finlay watched from the pram, his big round eyes going *very* wide.

Ruaridh checked the bush from top to bottom.

No fire here either!

![[Illustration: Ruaridh hosing rose bush, Finlay watching wide-eyed from pram]](./images/spread-3.png)

-----------

## Spread 4 — The Tall Tree

The tall tree at the back of the garden looked **very suspicious**.

Ruaridh soaked it **thoroughly**.

He checked left. He checked right.

Max gave one firm **Woof!** — just to be sure.

**All clear!**

![[Illustration: Ruaridh hosing big tree, checking left and right, Max barking]](./images/spread-4.png)

-----------

## Spread 5 — A Fire?!

"Ruaridh!" called Dad. "Come and look at this!"

There in the garden was a small campfire — crackling and glowing orange.

Ruaridh looked at it very carefully.

He held his hose tight.

He thought very, very hard.

Then he smiled — and put the hose down.

*This* fire was a good one.

![[Illustration: Ruaridh studying the campfire, hose in hand, smiling]](./images/spread-5.png)

-----------

## Spread 6 — Marshmallows

Mum handed out long sticks, each with a marshmallow on the end.

One for Ruaridh. One for Finlay. One for Mum. One for Dad. One for Max.

Can you count them? **One, two, three, four, five!**

They all huddled close around the warm, cosy glow.

The marshmallows turned golden and gooey.

Firefighter Ruaridh had kept the whole garden safe — and *this* was the very best reward.

![[Illustration: Family around campfire toasting marshmallows on sticks at dusk]](./images/spread-6.png)
